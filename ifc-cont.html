CORE PILLARS

1. The Word of God:

"The grass withers, the flower fades, but the Word of our God stands forever" (Isaiah 40:8). At IFC, the Word is our foundation. We are committed to the faithful teaching, study, and application of Scripture as God's ultimate authority, equipping believers to walk in truth and become ambassadors of Christ in a world that desperately needs Him.

2. Prayer:

We believe that prayer moves the hand of God and transforms the hearts of men. Inspired by James 5:16, "The effective, fervent prayer of a righteous man avails much," we gather in fervent intercession for spiritual revival, personal breakthroughs, and the advancement of God's Kingdom across the globe. Prayer is the heartbeat of IFC, shaping every aspect of our ministry.

3. Worship:

Worship is not just an act but a lifestyle. In spirit and truth (John 4:23-24), we lift up the name of Jesus Christ, inviting His presence into our midst. Through worship, we encounter God's holiness, receive His healing touch, and are renewed in His love, aligning our hearts with His divine purposes.

4. Fellowship:

We are one body in Christ (Romans 12:5), called to love, support, and edify one another. Our fellowship transcends denominational boundaries, fostering unity within the Body of Christ. Through gatherings, small groups, and spiritual mentoring, we cultivate a family atmosphere where every believer feels valued and strengthened in their faith journey.

5. Evangelism:

Jesus' final command—"Go and make disciples of all nations" (Matthew 28:19)—is the heartbeat of our mission. We are committed to reaching the unreached, both locally and globally, with the message of salvation. Through street evangelism, outreach events, and missions, we carry the light of Christ into the darkest corners of the earth.

Our Commitment

Practical Expressions of Our Ministry: Times of refreshing in God's presence, dedicated to intercession and spiritual renewal.

Community Outreach: Feeding the hungry, clothing the poor, and sharing the Gospel with those in need.

Discipleship Programs: Equipping believers to grow in their faith and live out their calling.

Global Missions: Partnering with missionaries and ministries to take the Gospel to unreached regions.

Practical Expressions of Our Ministry

Prayer Retreats: We stand firm on the authority of Scripture as the inerrant and inspired Word of God, guiding every aspect of our ministry.

Holiness & Purity: As a bride prepares for her wedding day, so we prepare ourselves in holiness for Christ's return, rejecting sin and pursuing righteousness (2 Corinthians 7:1).

Spirit-Empowered Living: We depend on the power of the Holy Spirit for boldness, guidance, and transformation (Acts 1:8).

Kingdom Advancement: Living out core beliefs with honesty, transparency, and a deep commitment to doing what is right.

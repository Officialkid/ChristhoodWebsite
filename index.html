Welcome to

CHRISTHOOD INTERNATIOAL OUTFIELD MINISTRY
(C.I.O.M)

At Christhood, our vision is to be the hands and feet of Christ, moulding lives through outreach, missions, mentorship, counselling, empowerment, and theological training. We are called to serve with unwavering compassion, guiding individuals into their divine purpose while reflecting the heart of Christ in every endeavour.

In a world longing for hope, we stand as beacons of God's grace—offering healing to the broken, guidance to the lost, and restoration to the weary. Whether through community support, mentorship, evangelism, or missions, we are committed to equipping and empowering you to walk boldly in your calling. As we serve together, let us embrace the power of unity, lifting one another in faith and love.

With hearts full of gratitude, we thank you for your continued prayers and support. We are deeply honoured to journey alongside you, speaking life, hope, and revelation into your spirit. In this house, you are seen, valued, and cherished. May Christ's rest renew you, His mercy move you, and His love lead you always.

JEWELS PROGRAM

Teen Life Mentorship

It is a countrywide mentorship program that midwifes the transitions from highschool with an aim of fine tuning intellectual young minds as they journey into a new territory of life. This program takes up the plethora of questions circling around members of this active age group and guides them through as per the principles of Scripture.

Branch Program

Traversing the country from region to region , an indistinguishable line of characters is notable among youths in the spaces of Universities and colleges . Under this program the trajectory is to create a honed desire , so as to form a homogeneous motive among these young minds.

Impact Program

An intriguing sequence of classes that aims in building up many through Knowledge , so much so as to polish their understanding of that which Scripture entails . It is an outreach training kit for equipping Saints for ministry by enhancing their Faith and Knowledge in Christ.

    About Jewels Ministry

    The Jewels Kids Mentorship Program is a transformative initiative designed to provide children aged 7 to 12 years with a holistic, faith-based enrichment experience outside the classroom. Rooted in Christian principles, this program seeks to empower, equip, and inspire young minds through mentorship, life skills training, educational support, and recreational activities.

    In an era of rapid social and technological change, the program aims to instill strong values, leadership skills, and resilience in the next generation.

    Childhood Development (Ages 7–12)

    "Can I make it in the World of People and Things?"

    During this stage (ages 7-12), children are capable of learning, creating, and accomplishing numerous new skills, developing a healthy sense of industry. This is a crucial social stage of development where unresolved feelings of inadequacy can lead to problems with competence and self-esteem.

    Social Development

    As their world expands, school and neighborhood relationships become significant alongside family.

    Skill Building

    The fundamentals of technology and life skills are developed during this critical stage.

    Encouragement

    When praised for accomplishments, children demonstrate industry and perseverance.

    Our Program Objectives

    Faith-Based Growth

    Instilling Christian values and spiritual development through Bible-based activities.

    Life Skills Development

    Teaching practical skills for daily living and future independence.

    Academic Support

    Providing educational assistance and STEM engagement activities.

    Character Building

    Developing leadership qualities and strong moral character.

    Talent Development

    Nurturing artistic, athletic, and creative talents through diverse activities.

    Community Engagement

    Fostering social responsibility and service to others.

    Our Unique Approach

    What We Avoid

    • Focusing on dysfunction
    • Negative thinking patterns
    • Increasing anxiety/depression

    What We Provide

    • Guidance for current challenges
    • Actionable life improvement
    • Transformational mentoring

    We partner with children to determine not what's wrong with them, but what they truly want in life and how to achieve it through friendly, personal and professional mentoring rooted in Christian values.

    Our Program Components

    Mentorship Sessions

    Features:

    • One-on-one Christian mentorship
    • Group discussions
    • Faith-based counseling

    Children build relationships with caring Christian adults who provide guidance and support.

    Life Skills Development

    Skills Developed:

    • Communication
    • Problem-solving
    • Financial literacy
    • Emotional intelligence

    Practical skills training to prepare children for real-world challenges.

    STEM & Academic Support

    Activities:

    • Homework assistance
    • Science experiments
    • Technology workshops
    • Math challenges

    Engaging educational activities to boost academic performance.

    Recreational Activities

    Options:

    • Sports and games
    • Arts and crafts
    • Music and drama
    • Field trips

    Fun activities to nurture talents and promote physical health.

    Our Delivery Methods

    Physical Mentorship Camps

    Immersive in-person experiences with hands-on activities and team building.

    Community Hubs

    Local gathering places for ongoing mentorship and activities.

    Online Platforms

    Virtual sessions and resources for remote participation.

    Field Trips & Outreach

    Educational excursions and community service projects.

    Program Benefits

    Kids matched with caring Christian adults for stability

    Understanding and using personal potential

    Development of individual service plans

    Setting short and long term goals

    Ongoing study and training activities

    Strong foundation in Christian faith and values

    Open and honest communication

    Encouragement to achieve goals

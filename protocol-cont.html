Protocol, Security & Hospitality Ministry

"Let All Things Be Done Decently and in Order"

Ministry Structure & Roles

1. Protocol Department

Objective: To coordinate the logistics, movement, and welfare of pastors, guest ministers, and other special guests.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Plan and manage the arrival, seating, and departure of ministers and dignitaries
  • Ensure clear communication between departments for smooth operations
  • Arrange transport, accommodations, and necessary materials for guest ministers

2. Security Department

Objective: To maintain a secure and safe environment for the church, its leaders, and congregation.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Secure the church premises before, during, and after services
  • Control entry and access to restricted areas
  • Escort and protect guest ministers where needed
Operational Guidelines

Before the Service or Event

Protocol Team: Confirm guest ministers' arrival times and logistics

Security Team: Conduct a pre-service security check of the premises

Hospitality Team: Set up refreshments and welcome areas

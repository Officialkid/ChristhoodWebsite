University Mentorship Program

IMPACT

Mission & Vision

Mission Statement: IMPACT is a comprehensive university mentorship program designed to equip the saints for ministry by deepening their faith and knowledge of Christ, enabling them to engage in impactful ministry and achieve holistic growth. Drawing inspiration from 2 Timothy 2:1-2, the program emphasizes intentional discipleship, spiritual transformation, and life application through authentic relationships and a Christ-centered community.

Vision: Our vision is to create a spiritually vibrant campus environment where students thrive in their relationship with Christ, grow as servant leaders, and make a transformative impact on their communities. IMPACT aims to nurture believers who are spiritually mature, firmly rooted in biblical truth, and equipped to disciple others, extending God's kingdom on and beyond the campus.

Core Pillars

Discipleship

Intentional mentoring relationships fostered through trust, guidance, and accountability, leading to spiritual and personal growth.

Biblical Teaching

Systematic teaching of God's Word, emphasizing sound doctrine and its practical application in daily life.

Prayer

Cultivating a lifestyle of fervent, consistent prayer, both individually and corporately, as a cornerstone of spiritual vitality.

Community

Building a Christ-centered community where students can share openly, grow together, and encourage one another.

Leadership Development

Preparing participants to take on leadership roles with integrity, humility, and Christlike character.

Program Objectives

  • To deepen participants' relationship with Christ by fostering a habit of daily devotion and reflection.
  • To develop godly character and servant-leadership qualities through mentorship and discipleship.
  • To establish small groups as safe spaces for spiritual growth, mutual accountability, and encouragement.
  • To equip students to disciple others, ensuring the multiplication of spiritually mature leaders.
  • To instill a commitment to prayer, worship, and other spiritual disciplines as a foundation for ministry.
  • To provide practical tools and training for effective ministry, community engagement, and personal growth.

Conclusion

"You then, my son, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus. And the things you have heard me say in the presence of many witnesses entrust to reliable people who will also be qualified to teach others." – 2 Timothy 2:1-2 (NIV)

IMPACT exists to glorify God by raising up a generation of Christ-centered leaders who are passionate about transforming lives and communities for His kingdom. Through mentorship, intentional discipleship, and practical ministry opportunities, participants will be empowered to fulfill their God-given purpose and extend His love to the world.

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations..."

Matthew 28:19

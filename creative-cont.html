Creative Arts Ministry Program

Inspiring Beauty and Worship

The Creative Arts Ministry exists to glorify God through creativity, art, and design. Rooted in Psalm 139:13-14 ("For you created my inmost being; you knit me together in my mother's womb. I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful, I know that full well."), this ministry seeks to reflect the beauty of God's creation by designing worship environments that engage the hearts, minds, and souls of the congregation.

Program Structure

The Creative Arts Ministry is divided into several key teams, each focusing on a specific aspect of the worship experience:

Visual Design Team

Mission: Create sermon series graphics, stage designs, and environmental décor that align with the message of the Gospel.

Responsibilities:

• Design visuals for sermon slides, social media, and print materials.

• Develop thematic stage designs that enhance the worship message.

• Coordinate seasonal decorations for Easter, Christmas, and other special events.

Key Scripture: Exodus 35:35

Worship Technology Team

Mission: Provide seamless sound, lighting, and media to enhance worship.

Responsibilities:

• Operate soundboards and ensure clarity in music and spoken word.

• Program and run lighting that complements the worship atmosphere.

• Manage live streaming and video recording for online services.

Key Scripture: Colossians 3:23

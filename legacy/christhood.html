    • CHRISTHOOD MINISTRIES

    Christhood Outfield International Ministries (CIOM) was birthed in 2009 by a passionate group of founders, driven by a deep concern for the spiritual and physical well-being of individuals. Beginning as a humble outreach initiative, CIOM's mission centered on sharing the transformative Gospel of Jesus Christ and providing practical support to the most vulnerable members of society—particularly women in impoverished communities. We sought to equip these women with the skills and resources necessary to care for their families, enabling them to thrive in difficult circumstances.

    Officially launched on November 1, 2015, CIOM began with six foundational programs: Teen Life, IFC, Impact, Branch, Sister Sister, and BOKS. These programs were designed to reach diverse groups, fostering growth through mentorship, empowerment, and community support. From the outset, our focus was on outreach, mission work, counselling, team-building, and training, grounded in the heart of service and compassion.

    Over the years, CIOM has experienced tremendous growth and expansion. Today, we operate twelve vibrant ministries, five dynamic programs, and three impactful humanitarian activities, each uniquely designed to meet the diverse needs of the communities we serve. Our commitment to transforming lives through practical, hands-on support and spiritual nurturing has deepened, impacting not only individuals but entire communities.

    As we look to the future, we continue to pray and expect God to work in mighty ways, confident in His power to lead and guide us. Our unwavering hope is captured in the words of Jude 1:24-25:

    "Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling, and to make you stand in the presence of His glory blameless with great joy,"—to Him, we humbly commit ourselves and the outcomes of our service, trusting in His faithfulness to continue the work He has begun.

    How We Live It Out

    At CIOM, we're committed to:

    • Living what we preach — with integrity, authenticity, and purpose.
    • Serving together as one family — across tribes, stories, and generations.
    • Discipling and equipping — because ministry is people, not platforms.
    • Growing in love and truth — so we reflect Christ, not just speak of Him.

    You'll find us organizing prayer session, holding Bible studies, fellowships, mentoring youth, supporting families, and stepping into broken places with the light and hope of Jesus.

    Why It Matters

    Because we've seen what God can do. He heals. He restores. He gives purpose. And we're here to be part of that story—in real lives, in real time.

    Whether you're searching, serving, or just starting to ask questions—you're welcome here. Jesus is at the center of all we do, and we believe He's not done yet.

    Come Walk with Us

Christhood Apologetics

Defending the Faith in the 21st Century

The Role of Good Philosophy in the Christian Mission

C.S. Lewis reminds us that the battle of ideas is central to the Christian mission. In an age dominated by secularism, relativism, and naturalism, Christians are called to engage thoughtfully and robustly, providing answers that affirm the credibility and relevance of our faith in Christ.

The Challenge of Secular Worldviews

Atheism

Denying the existence of God, atheism claims human life is the product of blind chance, stripping existence of inherent purpose.

This worldview falters when explaining objective moral values, the origin of consciousness, and humanity's innate longing for transcendence.

Scientism

A reductionist belief that empirical science alone is the arbiter of truth.

While science is a valuable tool for exploring nature, it cannot resolve ultimate purpose, morality, or the metaphysical. Faith and reason are allies.

Relativism

The belief that truth is subjective and morality is merely personal preference.

Christianity provides the objective moral framework necessary for genuine justice, human rights, and enduring truth.

Postmodern Skepticism

Questioning whether any worldview can claim universal truth.

The Gospel of Jesus Christ does not oppress but liberates, uniting humanity under God's redemptive love and historical truth.

The Need for Apologetics in Evangelism

Modern culture poses difficult questions about suffering, morality, and the exclusivity of Christ. Apologetics provides thoughtful and biblically grounded answers.

Many secular claims rest on internal contradictions. Apologetics helps uncover these inconsistencies with clarity and grace.

From the historical resurrection of Jesus Christ to the fine-tuning of the cosmos, the Christian worldview is intellectually robust.

Christianity speaks directly to deepest human longings—identity, purpose, reconciliation, and hope for eternity.

Undergirding Truths for the Christian Apologist

The Lordship of Christ

Jesus is the embodiment of truth (John 14:6). He alone fulfills humanity's need for salvation and purpose.

The Reliability of Scripture

The Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God, historically grounded and spiritually transformative.

The Historical Resurrection

The resurrection of Jesus is the cornerstone of Christian faith and the victory over sin and death.

Human Longing for God

Every soul carries a longing for meaning, justice, and love that only Christ can satisfy.

Conclusion

"But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts, and always be ready to give a defense to everyone who asks you a reason for the hope that is in you, with meekness and fear." — 1 Peter 3:15

Apologetics is not about winning arguments, but winning hearts and minds for Christ. In the 21st century, it stands as an indispensable tool for equipping believers and reaching thinkers.

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations..."

Matthew 28:19

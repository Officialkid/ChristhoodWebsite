Missions Ministry

As followers of Jesus Christ, we are called to be His hands and feet in a broken world, sharing His love, hope, and healing. This program outlines an efficient, God-centered, and impactful approach to missions and outreach, reflecting Christ's commission to His church to go into all the world and make disciples (Matthew 28:19-20).

Core Objectives

1. Proclaim the Gospel: Share the Good News of Jesus Christ with all people, prioritizing unreached and underserved communities.

2. Discipleship: Equip believers to grow in their faith, apply biblical principles, and share Christ with others.

3. Practical Support: Address physical, emotional, and social needs as a reflection of God's love and care.

4. Community Engagement: Foster unity and transformation within local communities through active involvement.

5. Prayer and Intercession: Seek God's guidance and power through consistent prayer, relying on the Holy Spirit to move in every aspect of the ministry.

Program Components

• Evangelism and Outreach

• Discipleship and Mentorship

• Counselling and Support Services

• Community Development

• Prayer Ministry

Connect With Us!

1. Collaborative Partnerships

Form partnerships with local churches, community leaders, and ministries to expand our reach.

Partner with schools and mental health organizations to offer joint initiatives that cater to the emotional and spiritual needs of students and families.

2. Faith-Based Volunteering

Equip volunteers with the knowledge, training, and spiritual grounding to support the mission effectively. Volunteers will be trained in spiritual care, active listening, and emotional support techniques rooted in Biblical principles.

3. Training & Development

Host workshops and seminars for those seeking to become more active in outreach, focusing on spiritual growth, emotional health, and effective outreach techniques.

Provide leadership training for those called to lead others in faith.

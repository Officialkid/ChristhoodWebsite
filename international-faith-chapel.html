IFC (International Faith Chapel)

Fellowship & Evangelism

IFC, an interdenominational prayer fellowship, called and commissioned by God to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ with power and to prepare His Church for His glorious return. Rooted in the Great Commission (Matthew 28:19-20) and inspired by the hope of the Bridegroom's return (Revelation 19:7), our ministry exists to transform lives through the Word, intercession, worship, and intentional fellowship.

Our Vision

To raise up a Christ-centered fellowship where believers are sanctified, equipped, and empowered to reflect His glory and prepare for His imminent return. We envision a community ablaze with the fire of the Holy Spirit, boldly advancing the Kingdom of God in every sphere of life.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, and mobilize them for ministry through fervent prayer, Spirit-led worship, intentional fellowship, and effective evangelism. We exist to glorify God by building a Church that is holy, united, and mission-focused, eagerly awaiting the Bridegroom's return.

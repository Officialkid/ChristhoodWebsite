BRANCH Mentorship Program

An exciting and interactive initiative dedicated to supporting high school graduates as they transition to college or university.

With a focus on career development, spiritual growth, leadership development, and holistic personal growth, BRANCH aims to empower young adults with the skills and virtues necessary for success in their academic and professional journeys.

The most important thing to remember is that mentoring youth isn't another meeting or program; it's a relationship.

Vision Statement

To empower young adults to transition confidently from high school to college/university while fostering personal growth, academic readiness, leadership skills, and faith-based values.

Mission Statement

To provide holistic mentorship rooted in practical skills, intellectual development, leadership training, and spiritual grounding, preparing participants for a purposeful and impactful college/university experience.

About Nawiri Africa

Established in January 2010, Nawiri Africa is a dynamic and results-driven training service provider, mentorship hub, and events management firm. With a strong foundation in the training industry, we specialize in delivering high-quality, cost-effective solutions tailored to meet the evolving needs of individuals, teams, and organizations.

At Nawiri Africa, we take pride in our team of skilled and experienced professionals who bring innovation, energy, and strategic insights into every engagement. Our rapid growth is fuelled by a dedication to excellence and a client-centric approach, enabling us to build strong partnerships across diverse industries including Government, Banking, Corporate Organizations, Religious Groups, and Educational Institutions.

Target Audience

The program is tailored for recent high school graduates (ages 17-19) who are preparing to join college or university.

AP: DENNIS G VINCENT

Husband | Father | Gospel Minister | Missionary | Apologist | Life Coach | Counsellor | Author | Certified Trainer | Visionary Leader

Ap Dennis N. Vincent is a devoted husband to Daisy, a dynamic gospel minister, and a visionary leader driven by a passion for empowering individuals and transforming communities through Christ-centered living. With over two decades of experience spanning ministry, training, and leadership development, Apostle Dennis combines theological depth with practical expertise to inspire and equip others to fulfil their God-given potential.

Through C.I.O.M, Apostle Dennis channels his passion for missions and outreach into actionable programs that make a lasting impact. The ministry is rooted in three key pillars:

01

Evangelism and Missions

+

Organizing gospel-centered campaigns that reach underserved and unreached communities with the message of Christ.

02

Leadership Development

+

Training emerging leaders in churches, ministries, and communities to thrive in their calling and lead with integrity.

03

Humanitarian Outreach

+

Providing practical support through initiatives like food distribution, educational resources, and healthcare support to uplift vulnerable populations.

C.I.O.M has touched lives across borders, transforming individuals and communities with a vision to foster faith, hope, and love.

Core Values

• Faith in Action: Upholding Christ as the cornerstone of every endeavour and demonstrating His love through service.

• Excellence in Execution: Delivering work that is not only impactful but also of the highest professional and ethical standard.

• Empowerment through Education: Equipping individuals with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to excel in their roles and callings.

• Integrity and Authenticity: Living out core beliefs with honesty, transparency, and a deep commitment to doing what is right.

Notable Accomplishments

Successfully authored the book *Supernatural Living* and the article series *Slice of Reality*, which explore topics ranging from Christian apologetics to leadership development.

Designed and facilitated high-impact training programs that have equipped hundreds of leaders and professionals across the corporate, religious, and non-profit sectors.

Led mission teams to underserved regions, impacting children, teenagers, youth, men, and women of all tribes, cultures, races, social statuses, and faiths..

Served as a keynote speaker in conferences and seminars addressing topics on faith, leadership, and personal growth.

Vision And Inspiration

Dennis N. Vincent is driven by a singular purpose: to glorify God through a life of service, leadership, and transformation. His ministry, career, and personal life are underpinned by a deep-seated belief in the power of faith to inspire change, uplift humanity, and foster a future defined by hope and love. Whether leading missions, coaching individuals, or shaping organizations, Dennis is steadfast in his mission to make a difference—one life, one community, and one vision at a time

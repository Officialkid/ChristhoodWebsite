Program Overview

The BRANCH Mentorship Program is an exciting and interactive initiative dedicated to supporting high school graduates as they transition to college or university. With a focus on career development, spiritual growth, leadership development, and holistic personal growth, BRANCH aims to empower young adults with the skills and virtues necessary for success.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long is the Branch Mentorship Program?
The Branch Mentorship Program is 12 weeks, sessions are once a week.
2. Is the Branch Mentorship Program physical or online?
The program is currently physical.
3. Is the registration fee of 3,000 Ksh per session?
The registration fee is for the entire program.
4. Do I have to pay the registration fee immediately?
No, you do not have to pay the registration fee immediately. First payment is a commitment fee of 1,000 Ksh within 14 days from the beginning of the program, and the balance before the end of the program. Parent/guardian can make a one-off payment.
5. Apart from registration fee, are there other expenses that we would need to cater for?
Yes, we have lunch after every session, outdoor sessions and community service. We kindly request you to support your child with the amount required to ensure everything is a success.
6. Is the Branch Mentorship Program Launch for the ex-candidates alone?
The program launch is for both ex-candidate and parent/guardian. We encourage the parent/guardian to attend to get the complete overview of the program, meet the facilitators, and have any arising question addressed.
7. What’s the age bracket for Branch Mentorship Program?
The age bracket is 17-19 years.
8. When and what time is the Branch Mentorship Program Launch?
The Branch Mentorship Program 2026 Launch is on 28th February 2026, 10 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. at Azuri Premier Hotel, Aga-Khan Walk, behind Co-op Bank House.

Counselling and Discipleship Program

Walking in Wholeness

Through our Counseling and Discipleship Program, we aim to reflect the heart of Christ in every interaction, providing a pathway to spiritual and emotional wholeness. Whether you are navigating personal struggles, relational conflicts, or a desire to grow deeper in your faith, we are here to walk alongside you.

Our Mission

Our mission is to walk alongside individuals in their journey toward spiritual and emotional health, offering Christ-centered guidance that equips them to live in alignment with God's purpose for their lives.

Read More
Program Objectives

1. Provide Biblical Guidance

Equip individuals with Scripture-based tools to address life's challenges and foster a deeper relationship with God.

2. Encourage Emotional Healing

Offer a safe space for individuals to explore their struggles and find healing through biblical counseling.

3. Foster Relational Restoration

Address interpersonal conflicts through Christ-centered mediation and discipleship.

4. Promote Discipleship Growth

Help individuals grow as disciples of Christ, living out their faith in practical and impactful ways.

5. Empower for Kingdom Impact

Equip individuals to minister to others, extending the cycle of healing and discipleship.

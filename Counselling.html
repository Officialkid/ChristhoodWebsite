Counselling & Discipleship Ministry

Walking in Wholeness, Healing, and Spiritual Maturity

Through our Counseling and Discipleship Program, we aim to reflect the heart of Christ in every interaction, providing a pathway to spiritual and emotional wholeness. Whether you are navigating personal struggles, relational conflicts, grief, or a desire to grow deeper in your walk of faith, we are here to walk alongside you.

Our Mission

To walk alongside individuals in their journey toward spiritual, psychological, and relational wholeness, offering Christ-centered guidance that equips them to live in alignment with God's divine purpose.

Program Objectives

1. Provide Biblical Guidance

Equip individuals with Scripture-based tools to address life's challenges and foster an authentic relationship with God.

2. Encourage Emotional Healing

Offer a safe, confidential space to address trauma, anxiety, and grief through prayer and pastoral counseling.

3. Foster Relational Restoration

Address marital, familial, and interpersonal conflicts through Christ-centered mediation and reconciliation.

4. Promote Discipleship Growth

Help believers establish spiritual disciplines, mind renewal (Romans 12:2), and biblical life habits.

5. Empower for Kingdom Impact

Equip individuals to minister comfort to others with the same comfort they received from God (2 Cor 1:3-4).

Counselling Framework

1. Initial Assessment & Intake

Each individual begins with a confidential session to understand their background, challenges, and goals, leading to a personalized discipleship plan.

2. Christ-Centered Therapy

Addressing emotional wounds, depression, spiritual doubts, and past trauma by applying Scripture, prayer, and cognitive renewal.

3. Discipleship Mentorship

Pairing participants with mature spiritual mentors for one-on-one encouragement, Bible study, and character formation.

4. Marriage & Family Guidance

Biblical premarital counseling, marital conflict resolution, and parenting guidance rooted in the Word of God.

5. Recovery & Wholeness Groups

Support circles for grief, addiction freedom, and life transitions in a loving, non-judgmental environment.

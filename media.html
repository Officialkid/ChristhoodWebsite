Media Ministry

Leveraging technology to spread the Gospel and enhance worship experiences

The Media Ministry is a vital component of the 21st-century church, leveraging technology to spread the Gospel, enhance worship, and foster engagement expanding outreach beyond the church walls.

Our Mission

To spread the Gospel and enhance the worship experience through audio, video, digital, and social media, ensuring that God's message reaches and impacts people wherever they are.

Key Objectives

1. Enhancing Worship

Creating an immersive, distraction-free environment that draws people into the presence of God.

2. Expanding Church Reach

Using online platforms to connect with those beyond the church building, including the sick, elderly, and global audience.

3. Improving Communication

Ensuring seamless internal and external communication through digital and multimedia tools.

4. Equipping Other Ministries

Providing resources and training to empower every church department to use media effectively.

Ministry Structure & Roles

1. Audio & Sound Engineering Team

Objective: Ensure clarity and excellence in sound for both in-person and online services.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Setup and operate sound equipment for services and events
  • Monitor and adjust audio levels during live services
  • Maintain and troubleshoot audio equipment

2. Visual Media & Presentation Team

Objective: Support worship and teaching through engaging visuals.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Create and display lyric slides for worship
  • Prepare sermon slides and visual aids
  • Operate presentation software during services
Importance in the 21st Century

Breaking Geographic Barriers

The church can now minister to people worldwide through live streaming and digital media.

Enhancing Engagement

Social media allows real-time interactions, questions, and discussions that keep members connected.

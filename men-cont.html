Men's Ministry Program

Holistic Program for Empowering, Equipping, and Discipling Men

Program Framework

Our comprehensive program is built on four key pillars that address every aspect of a man's life:

Pillar 1: Spiritual Growth and Discipleship

Goal: To deepen each man's relationship with God and develop a Christ-centered identity.

1. Foundational Bible Study

Weekly or biweekly small group sessions focusing on foundational doctrines and practical application.

2. Daily Devotion and Accountability

Introduce a daily devotion app or guide tailored for men, emphasizing Scriptures on identity and purpose.

3. Prayer and Worship Nights

Monthly gatherings for collective worship, testimonies, and intercession.

Pillar 2: Identity and Purpose Discovery

Goal: To help men understand their God-given identity and purpose.

1. Identity Workshops

Conduct immersive sessions on biblical manhood and countering cultural misconceptions.

2. Purpose Discovery Retreats

Quarterly retreats with focused sessions on discovering spiritual gifts and personal callings.

Implementation Process

1. Onboarding Process

Begin with a "Men's Launch Weekend" where new members learn the vision and core values.

2. Regular Check-Ins

Conduct biannual evaluations of each man's growth through one-on-one meetings.

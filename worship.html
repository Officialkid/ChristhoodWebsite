Worship Ministry

Christ-Centered Worship Ministry

The Christ-Centered Worship Ministry exists to inspire, uplift, and transform lives through creative expression. By blending faith, music, dance and drama, we aim to create an atmosphere where God's Word is experienced in a fresh and powerful way, making disciples and glorifying Jesus in all we do.

Our Mission

To inspire Christ-centered worship, cultivate creative expression, and deepen biblical understanding through the arts—drama, music, and dance—while fostering a purpose-driven commitment to serving our church and community with our God-given talents.

Read More
CORE VALUES

Christ-Centered Worship

Every artistic expression will be rooted in glorifying God and proclaiming His Word.

Biblical Foundation

Each performance and activity will be grounded in Scripture, ensuring spiritual growth and faithfulness to God's truth.

Excellence in Creativity

We will use our God-given talents to present powerful, spirit-filled performances that honour God.

Community and Service

Our ministry will reach beyond the church walls to serve and inspire others in our local community.

Discipleship and Growth

Encouraging participants to deepen their faith, develop their gifts, and grow in their walk with Christ.

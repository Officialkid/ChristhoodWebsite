Sister Sister Ladies Ministry

Raising women. Strengthening sisterhood. Impacting generations.

Founded in 2015 under the divine calling of Christhood International Outfield Ministries, Sister Sister is more than a ministry—it is a movement designed to raise, equip, and release a generation of virtuous women who understand their worth in Christ and champion unity through sisterhood.

Our Divine Purpose

Sister Sister was birthed with a clear mandate: to restore the God-ordained dignity, strength, and influence of womanhood. This ministry seeks to awaken the divine potential within every woman.

Read More
Our Key Focus Areas

1. Recognize Her Identity in Christ

We remind women that their worth is not determined by society but by the love and sacrifice of Jesus Christ.

2. Embody Godly Virtues

True feminine strength lies in virtues of humility, gentleness, wisdom, and faith - marks of divine power in action.

3. Build Lasting Bonds in Sisterhood

We create an environment of love, mutual support and accountability where women find not just friends but sisters.

4. Impact the World for Christ

We equip women to influence every sphere of society with grace, integrity and unwavering faith.

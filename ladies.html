Sister Sister Ladies Ministry

Raising women. Strengthening sisterhood. Impacting generations.

Founded in 2015 under the divine calling of Christhood International Outfield Ministries, Sister Sister is more than a ministry—it is a movement designed to raise, equip, and release a generation of virtuous women who understand their worth in Christ and champion unity through sisterhood.

Our Divine Purpose

Sister Sister was birthed with a clear mandate: to restore the God-ordained dignity, strength, and influence of womanhood. This ministry seeks to awaken the divine potential within every woman, deeply rooted in Proverbs 31.

Our Key Focus Areas

1. Recognize Her Identity in Christ

We remind women that their worth is not determined by society but by the love and sacrifice of Jesus Christ.

2. Embody Godly Virtues

True feminine strength lies in virtues of humility, gentleness, wisdom, and faith - marks of divine power in action.

3. Build Lasting Bonds in Sisterhood

We create an environment of love, mutual support, and accountability where women find not just friends but sisters in Christ.

4. Impact the World for Christ

We equip women to influence every sphere of society with grace, integrity, and unwavering faith.

Our Vision in Action

Mentorship for Legacy

Legacy mentors are paired with younger women to pass on godly wisdom and practical life skills, creating an unbroken chain of discipleship and empowerment.

Community Engagement & Support

Outreach initiatives empower women to address community needs, support families, and minister with practical compassion.

Spiritual Retreats & Gatherings

Regular fellowships, prayer retreats, and sisterhood circles where women can recharge, worship, and build deep spiritual connections.
